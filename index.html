About me

WHO I AM

I'm Jerry Shizeng, a freshman at the University of Southern California School of Cinematic Arts, studying Game Development and Interactive Design. I love Dark Souls, CRPGs, sandbox worlds, and any game that rewards patience, curiosity, and careful design.

Right now, I'm exploring level design, system design, and narrative design through small prototypes and student projects. I enjoy building worlds where the environment itself tells a story, and where every combat or puzzle encounter says something about the characters.

I'm always learning, always iterating, and always trying to make the next project a little more ambitious — and a little more fun to play.

Projects

Involution

An adventure game about exploring in an unknown world with the ability to control the vine. Players need to use the vine creatively in order to successfully explore through different levels. The vine can fit into various angle and terrain, serve as a tool to climb on. Players can also use the vine to destroy the trees that stand in their way. The process of designing this game is a unique experience, I learn about how to use Unity3D and how a simple mechanic like controlling the vine can be so amusing.

Explore Vinland

Explore Vinland is a board game about farming and competing. In this game, two players need to use their strategies to manager their farm and try to get a lot of money. There are various ways to combine the cards and create unique ways to get money. The order of the players is also imperative considering that there are four seasons with special buff within this game. Creating this board game is easy, but the process of playtesting it is hard. The experience of playtesting the game allows me to understand that controlling the balance between different players is difficult but necessary.

Demigod Dungeon

Demigod Dungeon is a team project that I made when I was in the NHSGA precollege program of Carnegie Mellon University. I am responsible for 2D environment art and 3D modelling. The experience of collaborating and teamworking is memorable for me. We combine the use of eye tracker into this game, allowing the players to use their eyes to deal with the enemies just like medusa. On the other hand, players navigate as the character Arachne, climb like a spider and shot web to jump around. I learn about texturing and making animations through the process of making the game.

Epiphany's Journey

This is a soul-like level design project about exploring through an island, fighting boss and finding treasures. Initially, I made the level design in Blender. However, in order to playtesting it, I moved it into Unreal Engine 5. There is an upper area and a lower area. In each area, there is only one bonfire. However, the bonfire can connect to 3-4 different path. There are 8 bosses within the game, only 4 of them are "real". Players need to defeat the real boss and destroy their statues in order to escape the island. I'm a serious fan of soul-like games, therefore making this project made me very glade and fulfilled.

The Lonely One

A 3D Scene I made in Blender. It's about a skeleton setting on a throne. The background story is about greedy prince who being banished by the king. Even after many years, he is still holding his treasure chest. I use different rocks to build the cave, and I use the texture paint to design the treasure chest. I also learn about how to use the material ball to adjust the atmosphere of the environment.

Game Jam

Good Night

This is an adventure game that I made with my friends at USC during the Ludum Dare 58 Event, and I was one of the artists. The theme for the game jam was “Collector”, which is why we made a game where the main character tries to collect items to unlock the hidden story that happens in his inner world.

Bubble Rapper

This is a rhythm game that our team “宇宙战舰队” made during the 2025 Global Game Jam, and I was the artist. The theme for the game jam was “Bubble”, which is why we created a rhythm game where you pop bubble wrap as a worker who dreams of being a rapper.

Hey

This is a two-player cooperative game that my teammates and I made during the 2024 Global Game Jam, and I was the designer. The theme for the game jam was “make me laugh”, so we made a funny game where the two players separately control an upper body and a lower body.

Deepseeker

Deepseeker is a roguelike game that I made with my team during the Ludum Dare 57 Event, and I was the designer/artist. The theme for the game jam was “Depths”, so we made a game about the depths of space and how we can seek within it.

The Point Club

The Point Club is a game that my teammates and I made during the Bitsy Micro Jam Game Memo 2025/4/24, and I was the artist/level designer. The game jam doesn’t have any theme restriction, but we are only allowed to develop on the platform Bitsy. We made a narrative game about Cybernetics with 3 different levels.
