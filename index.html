About me

WHO I AM

I’m Jerry Shizeng, a freshman in the University of Southern California School of Cinematic Arts, IMGD program. I love Dark Souls, CRPGs, sandbox worlds, and any game that rewards patience, curiosity, and careful design.

Right now I’m exploring level design, system design, and narrative design through small prototypes and student projects. I enjoy building worlds where the environment itself tells a story, and where every combat or puzzle encounter says something about the characters.

USC IMGD Freshman Level Design 3D & 2D Art Soulslike Enthusiast CRPG Lover

I’m always learning, always iterating, and always trying to make the next project a little more ambitious — and a little more fun to play.

Involution

Medium：Digital Game
Year：2024
Size：Digital
Description：An adventure game about exploring in an unknown world with the ability to control the vine. Players need to use the vine creatively in order to successfully explore through different levels. The vine can fit into various angle and terrain, serve as a tool to climb on. Players can also use the vine to destroy the trees that stand in their way. The process of designing this game is a unique experience, I learn about how to use Unity3D and how a simple mechanic like controlling the vine can be so amusing.

Explore Vinland

Medium：Board Game
Year：2024
Size：50cm x 50cm
Description：Explore Vinland is a board game about farming and competing. In this game, two players need to use their strategies to manager their farm and try to get a lot of money. There are various ways to combine the cards and create unique ways to get money. The order of the players is also imperative considering that there are four seasons with special buff within this game. Creating this board game is easy, but the process of playtesting it is hard. The experience of playtesting the game allows me to understand that controlling the balance between different players is difficult but necessary.

Demigod Dungeon

Medium：Digital Game
Year：2024
Size：Digital
Description：Demigod Dungeon is a team project that I made when I was in the NHSGA precollege program of Carnegie Mellon University. I am responsible for 2D environment art and 3D modelling. The experience of collaborating and teamworking is memorable for me. We combine the use of eye tracker into this game, allowing the players to use their eyes to deal with the enemies just like medusa. On the other hand, players navigate as the character Arachne, climb like a spider and shot web to jump around. I learn about texturing and making animations through the process of making the game.

Epiphany's Journey

Medium：Digital Game
Year：2023
Size：Digital
Description：This is a soul-like level design project about exploring through an island, fighting boss and finding treasures. Initially, I made the level design in Blender. However, in order to playtesting it, I moved it into Unreal Engine 5. There is an upper area and a lower area. In each area, there is only one bonfire. However, the bonfire can connect to 3-4 different path. There are 8 bosses within the game, only 4 of them are “real”. Players need to defeat the real boss and destroy their statues in order to escape the island. I’m a serious fan of soul-like games, therefore making this project made me very glade and fulfilled.
